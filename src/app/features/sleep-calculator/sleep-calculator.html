CIRCADIAN LIGHT

Sunlight is the important signal.

Morning and evening light can shift the timing of the circadian system. Your plan therefore shows sunrise, sunset and the surrounding natural-light window instead of pretending that bedtime is independent of the sky.

TRANSPARENT MATH

No fake precision.

Many calculators use a 90-minute cycle and a 15-minute fall-asleep allowance. We expose the allowance and show 4, 5 and 6-cycle alternatives instead of presenting one biological number as exact.
