The 90-Minute Ultradian Sleep Cycle

First identified by sleep researchers Eugene Aserinsky and Nathaniel Kleitman, healthy human sleep naturally cycles through NREM stages (N1, N2, N3 Slow-Wave) and REM (Dreaming) every 80 to 110 minutes (averaging 90 minutes). Waking up at the completion of a cycle rather than in the trough of Stage 3 Slow-Wave deep sleep eliminates sleep inertia (grogginess and cognitive disorientation).

Guidance based on American Academy of Sleep Medicine (AASM) and CDC adult sleep quotas. 2piClock is an educational circadian planning tool and does not provide medical diagnoses or replace clinical sleep study consultations.
